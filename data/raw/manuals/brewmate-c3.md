# Orbitra BrewMate C3 — User Manual

## Overview

The Orbitra BrewMate C3 is a fully automatic bean-to-cup coffee machine with a built-in conical
burr grinder and an automatic milk frothing system. It prepares espresso-based drinks and hot
water at the touch of a button.

## Technical specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Pump pressure | 19 bar |
| Water tank | 1.8 L, removable |
| Bean hopper | 250 g |
| Grounds container | up to 12 coffee pucks |
| Grinder | ceramic conical burr, 12 grind settings (1 = finest, 12 = coarsest) |
| Default grind setting | 6 |
| Power | 1450 W, 220–240 V |
| Heating time | about 40 seconds |
| Cup height | 75–140 mm (adjustable spout) |
| Weight | 8.2 kg |
| Connectivity | none (no Wi-Fi/app); USB-C service port for firmware updates |

## First use

1. Remove all packaging and rinse the water tank.
2. Determine your water hardness with the supplied test strip and set it in
   **Menu › Settings › Water hardness** (levels 1–4; default is 3).
3. Insert the PureFlow water filter (optional but recommended) — see *Water filter*.
4. Fill the water tank with fresh cold water up to the MAX mark and fill the bean hopper.
5. Switch the machine on with the power button. It heats up and performs an automatic rinse.
6. Prepare and discard the first two cups of coffee — the grinder needs to settle.

## Control panel and drinks

The touch panel has buttons for the following drinks (default volumes):

| Drink | Default volume |
|---|---|
| Espresso | 40 ml |
| Lungo | 110 ml |
| Americano | 40 ml espresso + 120 ml hot water |
| Cappuccino | 60 ml coffee + 120 ml milk foam |
| Latte macchiato | 40 ml coffee + 200 ml milk |
| Flat white | 80 ml coffee + 80 ml micro-foam (added in firmware 1.3.0) |
| Hot water | 200 ml |

* **Coffee strength** has 5 levels; press the bean icon to cycle through them.
* **Double cup**: press the drink button twice within 2 seconds.
* **Personalising volume**: press and hold a drink button for 3 seconds — the machine starts
  dispensing; press any button to stop when the cup has the desired amount. The new volume is saved.

## Adjusting the grinder

The grind knob is located inside the bean hopper. **Only turn the knob while the grinder is
running**, otherwise the grinder can be damaged. Turn it one step at a time and judge the result
after 2–3 cups.

* Coffee runs too fast / tastes watery → choose a finer setting (lower number).
* Coffee runs drop by drop / tastes bitter → choose a coarser setting (higher number).

## Milk system

1. Insert the milk tube into the milk outlet and place the other end into a milk carton or the
   Orbitra milk jug. Use cold milk (about 5 °C) for the best foam.
2. Select Cappuccino, Latte macchiato or Flat white.
3. **After every milk drink**, run the **Milk Clean** program: put a cup under the spout and press
   and hold the milk (cup) button for 3 seconds. The machine rinses the milk system with hot
   water and steam (about 30 seconds).
4. Once a week, disassemble the milk frother and wash its parts in warm water. The parts are also
   dishwasher safe (top rack).

## Brew group

The brew group can be removed through the service door on the right side.

* Rinse the brew group under lukewarm running water **once a week**. **Do not use detergent** and
  do not put it in the dishwasher.
* Let it dry completely before reinserting it.
* Lubricate the brew group with food-grade Orbitra lubricant every 500 cups or every 2 months.

## Water filter

* The optional **PureFlow** water filter reduces scale and improves taste.
* Soak a new filter in cold water for 30 seconds and screw it into the bottom of the water tank.
* Activate it in **Menu › Settings › Water filter**. With an active filter the machine reminds you
  to descale much less often.
* Replace the filter every **2 months or after 50 L** of water, whichever comes first.

## Descaling

Descaling removes limescale from the boiler and pipes. The machine displays the **Descale** icon
(and warning code C6) when descaling is due — typically every **3 months** with water hardness
level 3 and no filter.

1. Remove the PureFlow filter if installed.
2. Empty the drip tray and the grounds container.
3. Pour one bottle (250 ml) of **Orbitra DecalPro** descaling solution into the water tank and add
   water up to the MAX mark.
4. Place a container of at least 1.5 L under the coffee spout and the milk outlet.
5. Select **Menu › Maintenance › Descale** and follow the on-screen instructions.
6. The program takes about **25 minutes**, including two rinsing cycles. Do not interrupt it.

Use only Orbitra DecalPro or another descaler approved for fully automatic coffee machines.
Never use vinegar — it can damage the seals. Damage caused by limescale due to not descaling is not
covered by the warranty.

## Energy saving

* **Auto-off**: the machine switches off automatically after **30 minutes** of inactivity by
  default. Options: 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes (**Menu › Settings › Auto-off**).
* An automatic rinse is performed when the machine is switched on and before it switches off.

## Daily care

* Empty the drip tray and the grounds container when the indicator asks you to, or daily.
* Refill the water tank with fresh water every day.
* Wipe the coffee spout with a damp cloth.

## Safety notes

* Do not touch the spout and milk outlet during dispensing — risk of burns.
* Never fill the water tank with milk, sparkling water or hot water.
* Never put ground coffee, instant coffee or caramelised/flavoured beans in the bean hopper —
  it can block the grinder.
