# Orbitra RoboVac R7 — User Manual

## Overview

The Orbitra RoboVac R7 is a robot vacuum cleaner with a mopping module and LiDAR navigation.
It builds a map of your home, cleans room by room and returns to its charging dock automatically.
The robot is controlled with the buttons on its top cover or with the Orbitra Home mobile app.

## Technical specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Navigation | LiDAR laser distance sensor + 3D obstacle sensor |
| Maximum suction power | 4000 Pa |
| Battery | 5200 mAh Li-ion |
| Runtime | up to 150 min (Eco), 110 min (Standard), 70 min (Max) |
| Charging time | about 4.5 hours from empty to full |
| Dustbin capacity | 450 ml |
| Water tank capacity | 300 ml |
| Maximum climbing height | 20 mm (door thresholds, rug edges) |
| Noise level | 58 dB (Standard mode) |
| Wi-Fi | 2.4 GHz only (802.11 b/g/n); 5 GHz networks are not supported |
| Saved maps | up to 4 floor maps |
| Operating temperature | 0 °C to 40 °C |

## What is in the box

Robot, charging dock with power cable, mopping module with one washable mop pad, one spare
side brush, cleaning tool (with brush cutter blade), quick start guide.

## Buttons and indicators

* **Power / Clean button** — press to start or pause cleaning; press and hold for 3 seconds to
  switch the robot on or off.
* **Home button** — press to send the robot back to the dock.
* **Status light ring:**
  * solid white — ready / cleaning;
  * pulsing white — charging;
  * solid orange — low battery (below 20%);
  * blinking red — error, the robot also announces the error code by voice and in the app;
  * blinking blue — Wi-Fi setup mode.

## Installation of the charging dock

1. Place the dock on a hard, level floor against a wall.
2. Keep at least 0.5 m of free space on both sides of the dock and 1.5 m in front of it.
3. Do not place the dock in direct sunlight or near stairs.
4. Plug in the power cable. The dock indicator lights up white.
5. Place the robot on the dock manually before the first use and let it charge fully (about 4.5 hours).

## Connecting to the Orbitra Home app

1. Install the Orbitra Home app and create an account.
2. Make sure your phone is connected to a **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi network.
3. Press and hold the **Power** and **Home** buttons together for **5 seconds** until you hear
   "Wi-Fi reset" and the light ring blinks blue.
4. In the app tap **Add device › RoboVac R7** and follow the instructions.

If pairing fails, see the section *Wi-Fi connection problems*.

## Cleaning modes

* **Auto clean** — cleans the whole map room by room.
* **Room clean** — cleans selected rooms (app only).
* **Spot clean** — cleans a 1.5 m × 1.5 m square around the robot; press and hold the Home button
  for 3 seconds to start.
* **Suction levels** — Eco, Standard, Max. Default is Standard.
* **Carpet boost** — when a carpet is detected the robot automatically switches to Max suction.
  Carpet boost can be disabled in the app.

## Mopping

1. Fill the water tank (300 ml) with clean, cold tap water. Do not use hot water, detergents or
   essential oils — they can damage the pump and void the warranty.
2. Attach the mop pad to the mopping module and click the module onto the back of the robot.
3. The robot detects the module automatically and starts vacuuming and mopping at the same time.
4. Water flow has three levels (Low, Medium, High) and can be changed in the app.
5. When the mopping module is installed, the robot avoids carpets automatically
   (*carpet avoidance*, available since firmware 2.3.0).

Remove the mopping module after each mopping session and empty the water tank.

## Maps, no-go zones and schedules

* The robot creates a map during the first Auto clean. Up to **4 floor maps** can be saved
  (multi-floor support).
* In the app you can split or merge rooms, rename them and add **no-go zones** and **no-mop zones**.
* Up to 10 cleaning schedules can be created in the app.
* **Do Not Disturb** is enabled by default from **22:00 to 08:00**: the robot does not start
  scheduled cleaning and voice prompts are muted. The period can be changed or disabled in the app.

## Maintenance

| Part | Cleaning | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Dustbin | empty after every cleaning | — |
| Filter (HEPA) | tap out dust weekly; **do not wash** | every 3 months |
| Main brush | remove hair weekly with the cleaning tool | every 6–12 months |
| Side brush | clean monthly | every 3–6 months |
| Mop pad | wash after every use (hand or machine wash at max 40 °C) | every 3 months |
| Sensors (cliff sensors, LiDAR window, charging contacts) | wipe with a dry soft cloth monthly | — |
| Wheels | remove hair and dirt monthly | — |

The app shows the remaining life of each consumable and reminds you when a replacement is due.
After replacing a part, reset its counter in **App › Device › Maintenance**.

## Battery care

* If the robot is not used for a long period, charge it fully and switch it off; recharge at least
  every 3 months.
* Do not leave the robot with an empty battery for a long time, it may damage the battery.
* The battery may only be replaced by an Orbitra authorized service center.

## Wi-Fi connection problems

* The robot supports **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only**. If your router uses one network name for 2.4 and 5 GHz
  (band steering), temporarily create a separate 2.4 GHz network or disable 5 GHz during setup.
* Move the robot and dock closer to the router during setup.
* Network names and passwords with special characters such as `&` or `\` may cause pairing to fail.
* Repeat the Wi-Fi reset (Power + Home for 5 seconds).

## Factory reset

A factory reset deletes all maps, schedules and settings. Open the top cover and use a pin to press
and hold the **reset button** (next to the dustbin) for **10 seconds** until the robot announces
"Restoring factory settings". The robot restarts in about one minute.

## Safety notes

* Remove cables, socks and small objects from the floor before cleaning.
* Do not use the robot outdoors, on wet floors or near open flames.
* Use only the original charging dock and power adapter.
