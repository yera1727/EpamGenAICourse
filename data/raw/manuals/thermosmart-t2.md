# Orbitra ThermoSmart T2 — User Manual

## Overview

The Orbitra ThermoSmart T2 is a Wi-Fi smart thermostat for low-voltage (24 V) heating and cooling
systems. It supports schedules, Eco mode, geofencing and remote control from the Orbitra Home app.

## Technical specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Supply voltage | 24 V AC (C-wire) |
| Compatible systems | 24 V gas/oil furnaces, boilers, central air conditioning, heat pumps (single-stage and two-stage) |
| Setpoint range | 5 °C to 35 °C, in 0.5 °C steps |
| Temperature sensor accuracy | ±0.5 °C |
| Humidity sensor accuracy | ±3% RH |
| Display | 2.4" colour display with rotating ring |
| Wi-Fi | 2.4 GHz (802.11 b/g/n) |
| Battery backup | none |

## Compatibility

* The T2 works with **24 V low-voltage** HVAC systems.
* It is **not compatible** with high-voltage (120/240 V) line-voltage systems such as electric
  baseboard heaters, and not compatible with millivolt systems (some gas fireplaces).
* A **C-wire (common wire)** is required to power the thermostat. If your system has no C-wire,
  use the **Orbitra Power Adapter PA-1** (sold separately), which is installed at the HVAC unit.
* Use the compatibility checker in the Orbitra Home app before installation.

## Installation

> Switch off power to the HVAC system at the breaker before removing your old thermostat.

1. Remove the old thermostat and take a photo of the wiring for reference.
2. Label the wires (R, C, W, Y, G, O/B) using the supplied stickers.
3. Mount the T2 base plate on the wall with the supplied screws and level.
4. Connect the wires to the matching terminals.
5. Attach the display to the base plate and switch the power back on.
6. Follow the on-screen setup wizard: language, units, system type and Wi-Fi.

## Controls

* **Rotate the ring** to change the target temperature.
* **Press the ring** to open the menu and confirm a selection.
* **Factory reset**: Menu › Settings › Reset › Factory reset, or press and hold the ring for
  **15 seconds**.

## Schedules

* Up to **6 temperature periods per day**, configured for each day of the week.
* Schedules can be created on the device or in the app.
* A manual temperature change is kept until the next scheduled period (temporary hold). A permanent
  hold can be set in the app.

## Eco mode

Eco mode saves energy when nobody is at home.

* Default Eco temperatures: **17 °C for heating** and **28 °C for cooling**. Both values can be
  changed in the app.
* Eco mode can be activated manually or automatically by geofencing.

## Geofencing

* Geofencing uses your phone's location to detect when the last household member leaves home and
  switches the thermostat to Eco mode; when someone approaches home it returns to the schedule.
* Default radius is **500 m**. Since firmware 3.1.0 the radius can be adjusted from **200 m to
  2000 m** in the app.
* All household members who should be considered must enable location permission in the app.

## Other features

* **HVAC filter reminder**: reminds you to replace the filter of your furnace/air conditioner every
  **90 days** (interval adjustable in the app).
* **Energy report**: monthly heating/cooling runtime statistics in the app.
* **Humidity display**: current indoor relative humidity is shown on the display.

## Wi-Fi

* The thermostat supports **2.4 GHz** networks only.
* To reconnect: Menu › Settings › Wi-Fi › Change network.
* If Wi-Fi is lost the thermostat keeps working with the last saved schedule; error H3 is shown.

## Safety notes

* Installation must be performed with the HVAC power switched off.
* If you are not sure about the wiring, have the thermostat installed by a qualified technician.
* Do not connect the T2 to 120/240 V wiring — this will destroy the device and is not covered by
  the warranty.
