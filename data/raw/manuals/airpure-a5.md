# Orbitra AirPure A5 — User Manual

## Overview

The Orbitra AirPure A5 is an air purifier with a three-stage filtration system and a laser PM2.5
sensor. It removes dust, pollen, pet dander, smoke and odours in rooms up to 45 m².

## Technical specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Recommended room size | up to 45 m² |
| CADR (particles) | 350 m³/h |
| Filtration | pre-filter + H13 HEPA + activated carbon |
| Sensor | laser PM2.5 sensor |
| Noise level | 22 dB (Sleep mode) to 52 dB (Turbo) |
| Power consumption | 45 W maximum, 1.5 W in standby |
| Wi-Fi | 2.4 GHz |
| Dimensions | 260 × 260 × 520 mm |
| Weight | 5.1 kg |

## Filtration stages

1. **Pre-filter** — captures hair, lint and large dust. It is washable.
2. **H13 HEPA filter** — captures 99.95% of particles down to 0.3 µm (pollen, fine dust, pet dander).
3. **Activated carbon layer** — absorbs odours, smoke and volatile organic compounds.

The HEPA and carbon layers form one combined **filter cartridge**.

## Before first use

1. Open the bottom cover by turning it counter-clockwise.
2. Remove the plastic wrapping from the filter cartridge — the purifier will not work properly
   with the wrapping on.
3. Insert the cartridge, close the cover and plug in the purifier.

## Controls

The touch panel on top has the following buttons: **Power**, **Mode**, **Speed**, **Timer**, **Lock**.

* **Mode** cycles between Auto, Sleep and Turbo.
* **Speed** sets manual fan speed 1, 2 or 3.
* **Timer** sets automatic switch-off after 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours.
* **Child lock**: press and hold **Lock** for **3 seconds** to lock or unlock all buttons.

## Operating modes

* **Auto** — fan speed is adjusted automatically according to the PM2.5 sensor reading.
* **Sleep** — the quietest mode (22 dB); the display and the light ring switch off.
* **Turbo** — maximum fan speed (52 dB) for fast cleaning, e.g. after cooking.
* **Manual** — fixed speeds 1–3.

## Air quality indicator

The light ring shows the current PM2.5 concentration:

| Colour | PM2.5 (µg/m³) | Air quality |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | 0–35 | Good |
| Purple | 36–75 | Moderate |
| Red | above 75 | Poor |

The exact value is shown on the display and in the Orbitra Home app.

## Maintenance

| Part | Action | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-filter | vacuum or rinse with water, let dry completely | every 2 weeks |
| Filter cartridge (HEPA + carbon) | replace (not washable) | every 6–12 months, or when the filter indicator lights up |
| PM2.5 sensor | clean the sensor lens with a dry cotton swab | every 2 months |
| Housing | wipe with a dry soft cloth | as needed |

The filter life is calculated from operating hours and fan speed. When less than 10% remains, the
filter icon blinks; at 0% the icon is solid and error **F4** is shown.

### Replacing the filter cartridge

1. Switch off and unplug the purifier.
2. Open the bottom cover, take out the old cartridge and insert a new genuine Orbitra
   A5 filter cartridge.
3. Close the cover and plug in the purifier.
4. **Reset the filter counter**: press and hold the **Timer** button for **5 seconds** until the
   filter icon goes off.

## App features

* Remote control of modes, speeds and timer.
* PM2.5 history for the last 30 days.
* Filter life percentage and reminders.
* Schedules (e.g. Sleep mode every night from 23:00).

## Safety notes

* Keep at least 30 cm of free space around the purifier.
* Do not use the purifier in bathrooms or other places with high humidity.
* Never use the purifier without a filter cartridge.
* Do not wash the filter cartridge — water destroys the HEPA and carbon layers.
