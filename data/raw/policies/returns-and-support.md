# Orbitra Home — Returns and Customer Support

## Right of return

* Products bought directly from the Orbitra online store can be returned within **30 days** of
  delivery without giving a reason.
* The product must be complete, undamaged and returned with all accessories. Original packaging is
  preferred.
* For hygiene reasons, a BrewMate C3 that has already been used to make coffee can only be returned
  under the warranty policy (if defective), not under the 30-day right of return.
* Products bought from other retailers must be returned to that retailer according to its policy.

## Refunds

* Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **14 days** after the returned product
  is received and inspected.
* Return shipping is free for defective products. For returns without a defect, the return shipping
  cost of 9.90 EUR is deducted from the refund.

## Contacting support

| Channel | Availability |
|---|---|
| Orbitra Home app (Profile › Support) | 24/7 ticket submission |
| E-mail: support@orbitra.example | answer within 1 business day |
| Phone hotline | Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 CET |
| Live chat on the website | Monday–Saturday, 8:00–20:00 CET |

Please have the **serial number** ready. It is printed on a label under the device (RoboVac R7: under
the dustbin; BrewMate C3: behind the drip tray; AirPure A5: on the bottom; ThermoSmart T2: on the
back of the display) and is also shown in the app under Device › Info.

## Spare parts and consumables

* Genuine consumables (filters, brushes, mop pads, PureFlow filters, DecalPro, lubricant) are sold in
  the Orbitra online store and in the app.
* Spare parts for all products are available for at least **7 years** after the end of production.

## Data and privacy

* Maps created by the RoboVac R7 and location data used for ThermoSmart T2 geofencing are stored
  encrypted and are never shared with third parties for advertising.
* You can delete your account and all device data in the app (Profile › Privacy › Delete account).
