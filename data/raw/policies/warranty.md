# Orbitra Home — Limited Warranty Policy

## Warranty period

* All Orbitra Home products (RoboVac R7, BrewMate C3, AirPure A5, ThermoSmart T2) come with a
  **2-year limited warranty** starting from the date of purchase by the first end customer.
* **Exception:** the rechargeable battery of the RoboVac R7 is covered for **1 year**.
* Repaired or replaced products are covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or
  for 90 days, whichever is longer.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use in accordance with the user
manual. Orbitra will, at its discretion, repair the product, replace it with a new or refurbished
product of equal value, or refund the purchase price.

## What is not covered

* **Consumables and wear parts**: filters (robot vacuum filter, AirPure filter cartridge, PureFlow
  water filter), brushes, mop pads, descaling solution, lubricant.
* Damage caused by not following the maintenance instructions, in particular **limescale damage**
  in the BrewMate C3 when descaling was not performed, or use of vinegar as a descaler.
* Damage caused by using hot water, detergents or oils in the RoboVac R7 water tank.
* Connection of the ThermoSmart T2 to 120/240 V wiring.
* Physical damage, liquid damage (except intended use), fire, power surges.
* Repairs or modifications made by anyone other than an Orbitra authorized service center.
* Commercial use (offices, cafés, hotels) — the products are intended for household use only.
* Cosmetic wear such as scratches.

## Proof of purchase

A valid proof of purchase (receipt or invoice showing the date, the seller and the product model)
is required for every warranty claim. Registering the product in the Orbitra Home app stores the
purchase date but does not replace the receipt.

## Orbitra Care+ extended warranty

* **Orbitra Care+** extends the warranty by **2 additional years** (total 4 years).
* It can be purchased in the Orbitra Home app or online **within 60 days** of the product purchase.
* Care+ also covers the RoboVac R7 battery for the full extended period.
* Care+ does not cover consumables or accidental damage.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Open a support ticket in the Orbitra Home app (**Profile › Support › New ticket**) or write to
   support@orbitra.example. Include the serial number, the problem description, the error code
   (if any) and the proof of purchase.
2. Support will try to solve the problem remotely first.
3. If a repair is needed, you receive a free shipping label. Pack the product in its original
   packaging or equivalent protective packaging.
4. Typical repair turnaround is **7–10 business days** after the product arrives at the service
   center.
